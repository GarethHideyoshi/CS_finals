FAQ

WHAT IS THE CURRENT STATE OF THE GAME?
+

// TRANSMISSION: ACTIVE COMBAT TESTING (V2.5)

Clear The Horde is currently in active Beta. Core survival loops, squad co-op, and horde AI are fully playable, while new maps, weapon classes, and boss mutations are deployed on a regular cycle. Expect frequent balance passes as we tune difficulty scaling.

HOW OFTEN DO PATCHES AND HOTFIXES DROP?
+

// DEPLOYMENT CADENCE

  • MAJOR CONTENT DROPS: Deployed every 3–4 weeks (new zones, weapons, and seasonal events).
  • BALANCE & AI PATCHES: Released bi-weekly based on telemetry and player feedback.
  • CRITICAL HOTFIXES: Deployed immediately whenever server stability or game-breaking bugs are detected.
WILL MY BETA PROGRESS BE WIPED AT LAUNCH?
+

// COMMAND DIRECTIVE: DATA RETENTION

While we strive to preserve all player progression, major infrastructure overhauls during Beta may require a partial or full reset of weapon loadouts and rank before v1.0. Any exclusive Beta tester badges, event skins, and hazard-stripe cosmetics unlocked during testing are permanently bound to your account.

HOW DO I REPORT CRASHES, GLITCHES, OR EXPLOITS?
+

// FIELD DIAGNOSTICS PROTOCOL

  • IN-GAME CONSOLE: Press F8 at any time during a match to capture a screenshot, your current coordinates, and server log automatically.
  • DISCORD & FORUMS: Submit video clips and crash logs in the #beta-bug-reports channel for direct triage by the dev team.
WHY ARE SERVERS OCCASIONALLY OFFLINE?
+

// SERVER MAINTENANCE & STRESS TESTING

Because the game is under continuous development, servers may go offline briefly for live database migrations, hotfix rollouts, or high-load horde stress tests. Check the News terminal for scheduled maintenance windows and real-time patch notes.
